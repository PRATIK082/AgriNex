# Code of Conduct
AgriNex is committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community.

Expected behavior: be respectful, constructive and patient; critique implementations rather than people; welcome questions; respect privacy, attribution, licenses and local farming knowledge; disclose conflicts of interest; prioritize farmer, worker, animal, environmental and public safety.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, threats, deliberate misinformation, plagiarism and knowingly unsafe instructions presented as validated guidance are unacceptable. Maintainers may moderate contributions and participation. Report concerns privately; handle reports confidentially and fairly.
