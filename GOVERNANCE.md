# Governance
Initial governance is maintainer-led with community input. Architecture decisions require ADRs. Agronomy features need qualified scientific review before being described as validated. Robotics/hardware needs competent engineering review and risk evidence. Data/model adoption requires license, consent, provenance and privacy review. Releases require tests and evidence.

Contributors retain copyright and submit contributions under the repository license subject to law and contribution terms. Do not submit work owned by employer/customer/university/third party without authorization. No feature may claim certification, production readiness or validated autonomous safety without independent evidence and required approvals.
