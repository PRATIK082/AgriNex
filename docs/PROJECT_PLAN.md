# Project Plan
Build the intelligence/integration/verification layer before custom machinery. Buy or partner for certified equipment in early pilots.

## Workstreams
Product discovery and farmer economics; agronomy/science; data/digital twin; AI/CV/forecasting; robotics/embedded/hardware; simulation/test/safety; market/supply chain; open-source governance.

## Pilot selection
Score crop/site 1–5 for value/ha, labor intensity, repeatability, mechanization fit, crop geometry, pest complexity, water, data/ground truth, market access, partner readiness, safety and season timing. Document weights with local experts.

## Pilot metrics
Matched baseline where practical; marketable yield/quality; water and input per marketable output; labor/machine hours; intervention rate; uptime/failures/crop damage; safety events; cost, usability and gross margin. Report uncertainty.

## Business hypotheses
Farm SaaS; drone scouting service; robot-as-a-service; FPO/custom-hiring fleet; irrigation automation; equipment integration; produce/storage/logistics marketplace; lawful aggregated data; finance/insurance partners. Validate willingness to pay and unit economics.
