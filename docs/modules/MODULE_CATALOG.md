# Module Catalogue
AGX-01 Farm Digital Twin; AGX-02 Agricultural Data Intelligence; AGX-03 AI Agronomist; AGX-04 Soil Intelligence; AGX-05 Land Preparation; AGX-06 Seeding and Planting; AGX-07 Irrigation; AGX-08 Nutrition/Fertigation; AGX-09 Crop Protection; AGX-10 Aerial Robotics; AGX-11 Ground Robotics; AGX-12 Crop Health; AGX-13 Harvest/Post-Harvest; AGX-14 Market Intelligence; AGX-15 Farm Autonomy Orchestrator.

Each module owns purpose/non-goals, requirements, interfaces, data contracts, units/CRS/time, state/error semantics, safety/security/privacy impacts, license/dependencies, tests, limitations, monitoring and maintenance. Shared capabilities include identity/tenancy, audit, geospatial, provenance, offline sync, device registry, notifications, observability and export.

## Cross-module capability: Soil, Seed, Leaf & Plant Intelligence
AGX-01 owns field/zone/crop-cycle identity; AGX-02 owns sensor/lab ingestion and provenance; AGX-03 synthesizes evidence and explains advisory; AGX-04 owns soil samples/results; AGX-06 owns seed lots/tests and planting; AGX-08 owns nutrition plans/application records; AGX-12 owns leaf imagery, tissue-linked observations and growth trends.
