## Research questions
1. What crop/site offers repeatable operations, measurable value and accessible ground truth?
2. What is the lowest-cost machine capable of safe field execution?
3. Which open-source projects can be integrated without license conflicts?
4. Which sensors provide reliable, calibrated measurements at farm economics?
5. What data can legally be used commercially and for model training?
6. What is the actual payback versus labor/custom-hiring alternatives?
7. What operating design domain can be validated safely?
