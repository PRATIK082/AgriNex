# Development Roadmap
Capability-based stages; dates are not guaranteed.

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Research and license audits; stakeholder interviews; choose one crop/site; requirements; hazard log; architecture/interface baseline; pilot metrics. Exit: reviewed scope and baseline.

## Phase 1 — Digital farm
Farm/field registry; GIS; crop cycle; input/observation records; weather and satellite ingestion; sensor gateway; offline capture; tenancy, audit and export. Exit: one field represented and audited.

## Phase 2 — Agronomy decision support
Crop/variety profiles; soil-test workflow; crop calendar; ET/water balance; pest/disease/weed scouting; evidence-bearing recommendations; agronomist approval; model cards. Exit: reviewed recommendations validated against observations.

## Phase 3 — Controlled automation
Irrigation test rig; independent safety controller; manual isolation; offline watchdog; drone imagery ingestion; device identity/OTA; fault injection. Exit: test-rig automation proven.

## Phase 4 — Robotics simulation
ROS 2 robot model; Gazebo world; localization/navigation; obstacles/fault scenarios; mission authorization; simulated scouting; HIL. Exit: reproducible simulation/HIL evidence.

## Phase 5 — Supervised field pilot
Site risk assessment/permissions; baseline; supervised scouting rover and irrigation; ground-truth crop/yield; reliability, water/input, cost and usability evaluation. Exit: independent review of measured results.

## Phase 6 — Expanded autonomy and market
Seeding/weeding/precision application integrations; harvest readiness/grading/storage; regional production/price forecasts; buyer/logistics; multi-farm fleet. Exit: validated multi-operation workflow with transparent economics and safety case.

## Success metrics
Yield/quality vs matched baseline; water per marketable yield; input use; machine availability and intervention rate; CV false positives/negatives; recommendation calibration; gross margin/payback; incidents/near misses; data completeness, uptime and offline recovery.
