## Mandatory safety principles
Independent physical E-stop; local safety controller; safe state on communication/localization/critical sensor loss; geofencing/exclusion zones; obstacle/person detection appropriate to risk; local watchdog; speed/force limits; manual isolation; spray interlocks/weather checks; trained authorized operators; audit and incident trail. Cloud AI/LLM must never bypass safety. Chemical application requires verified approved product/label, equipment compatibility, weather/drift/buffer controls, PPE, emergency response and legal permissions.

## Verification
Hazard log; operating design domain; risk assessment; safety requirements; architecture; FMEA/FMEDA as applicable; test evidence; operator training; maintenance and incident recovery. Bench → HIL → closed area → supervised field trial → limited operational domain. Simulation alone does not establish safety.
