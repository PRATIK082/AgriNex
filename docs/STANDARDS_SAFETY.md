## Mandatory safety principles
Independent physical E-stop; local safety controller; safe state on communication/localization/critical sensor loss; geofencing/exclusion zones; obstacle/person detection appropriate to risk; watchdog; speed/force limits; manual isolation; spray interlocks/weather checks; authorized operators; audit and incident trail. Cloud AI/LLM must never bypass safety. Chemical application requires approved product/label, equipment compatibility, weather/drift/buffer controls, PPE, emergency response and legal permissions.

Verification: hazard log, operating domain, risk assessment, safety requirements, architecture, FMEA/FMEDA as appropriate, tests, operator training, maintenance and recovery. Bench → HIL → closed area → supervised field trial → limited operating domain.
