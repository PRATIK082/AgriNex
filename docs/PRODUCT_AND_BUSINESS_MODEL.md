# Product and Business Model Hypotheses
Customers: small/medium farms, FPOs/cooperatives, large farms, custom-hiring centres, agronomists, universities, OEMs, drone providers, processors and buyers.

Models to validate: farm SaaS; drone-as-a-service; robot-as-a-service/shared fleet; FPO/custom-hiring management; irrigation automation; OEM integration/support; produce buyer/storage/processing/logistics marketplace; lawful aggregated forecasts; insurance/credit integrations with regulated partners.

Measure revenue per farm/acre/operation, utilization, operator/travel cost, battery/maintenance, depreciation, financing, insurance, support, connectivity, seasonality, risk and acquisition. Compare ownership, leasing, custom hiring and service models.

Validate payer, urgent pain, measurable savings/income, willingness to pay, service/repair capacity, off-season utilization, fragmented plot suitability and liability/insurance. Technical feasibility is not commercial viability.
