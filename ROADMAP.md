# AgriNex Roadmap
Capability-based stages; dates are not guaranteed.

1. **Foundation:** research/license audit, stakeholder interviews, crop/site selection, requirements, hazard log and architecture baseline.
2. **Digital farm:** farm/field registry, GIS, crop cycle, weather/satellite, sensors, offline capture, audit and export.
3. **Agronomy:** crop/variety, soil, crop calendar, water balance, scouting, evidence-bearing recommendations and expert review.
4. **Controlled automation:** irrigation test rig, independent safety controller, manual isolation, drone imagery and device security.
5. **Robotics simulation:** ROS 2/Gazebo, localization/navigation, obstacle/fault scenarios, mission authorization and HIL.
6. **Supervised pilot:** approved site, baseline, supervised rover/irrigation, crop/yield ground truth, reliability and cost evaluation.
7. **Expanded autonomy:** seeding, weeding, precision application, harvest, grading, storage and regional market intelligence.

Success metrics: yield/quality vs matched baseline; water/input per marketable yield; labor/machine hours; availability/intervention; CV false positives/negatives; recommendation calibration; gross margin/payback; safety events; data completeness and offline recovery.
